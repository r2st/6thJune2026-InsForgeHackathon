Why Coupa / SAP Ariba can't just ship this

Live-web licensing incumbents have ERP feeds, not Bright Data access · model economics Granite on MI300X is ~$0.02/supplier/day, GPT-4-class pricing would price them out of the mid-market · deterministic scoring the model is pinned to extraction; the math is auditable Python — incumbents ship LLM-judged scores their compliance teams already reject.
